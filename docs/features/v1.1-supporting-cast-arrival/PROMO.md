# Promo kit: v1.1.0 Supporting Cast Arrival

Everything needed to announce the release on social media. Rendered graphics are in
[`promo/`](promo); their source is `roadmap-video/src/promo/SupportingCast.tsx` (see
[How to re-render](#how-to-re-render)).

> **Art:** the cards show each unit's real card portrait from the game (`promo-sc.mjs` copies
> them each time it renders; a unit without a portrait falls back to its emblem).

## Store "What's new" text (v1.1.0)

> **Supporting Cast Arrival is here!**
> Eight new support units join Tower Rush. They never attack, but they change everything:
> - **Mime:** becomes a copy of a unit of the same rank.
> - **Portal Imp:** swaps places with a unit, or hops to an empty tile.
> - **Mirror Slime:** turns into a neighbour of the same rank.
> - **Lucky Cat:** merges next to it can keep their unit.
> - **Hourglass Owl:** neighbours charge their ultimate faster.
> - **Echo Spirit:** repeats a neighbour's ultimate.
> - **Banner Herald:** every awakened unit makes your whole army stronger.
> - **Gnome Brewer:** brews mana every few seconds and pays out every wave.
> Log in for a free Gnome Brewer and Portal Imp!

## Infomercial script (~24s, vertical)

Rendered as `promo/sc-teaser.mp4` (silent: add the game's battle theme in the posting app or an editor). Voice-over is
optional; the on-screen text tells the story on mute.

| Time | Picture | On-screen text | Voice-over (optional) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | The Tower Rush board in the crystal cave; famous heroes (Valkyrie, Fox Samurai, Crystal Queen) pop onto it. | "You know the heroes..." | "You know the heroes." |
| 3–6s | Card backs drop in a row as dark silhouettes with question marks. | "...but who has their back?" | "But who has their back?" |
| 6–8s | Flash of light, the title slams in. | **SUPPORTING CAST ARRIVAL** | "Supporting Cast Arrival!" |
| 8–18s | The 8 cards flip one by one (1.25s each), each with its verb: Copy, Swap, Mirror, Fortune, Overclock, Encore, War Cry, Brew. | Unit name + verb | "Copy. Swap. Mirror. Luck. Speed. Encore. War Cry. Brew." |
| 18–21s | All 8 cards fan out around the tagline. | "Not every hero swings a sword." | "Not every hero swings a sword." |
| 21–24s | Logo, version and call to action. | "v1.1 · Play now" | "Tower Rush, version one point one. Play now." |

## How to re-render

From `roadmap-video/`:

```bash
npm install
npm run promo:sc
```

The script copies the game art it needs from `game/public/assets` into `roadmap-video/public`
(git-ignored) and renders every file above into this folder's `promo/`. Preview and tweak in
Remotion Studio with `npm run studio`.
