## Discord patch notes (short)

**v2.9.0 League Revamp**
- Every league has a minimum wave: reach it to win trophies (no cap), fall short to lose them

- New badges for Mythic and Immortal

## Team-only facts

- Secret Society League: 30,000 trophies plus 5,000,000 gold and 10,000 gems held at once, entry
  fee paid once, then 30,000 gold and 1,000 gems every 30 days. Missing dues or dropping below
  30,000 trophies ends membership. Members show the Society badge on the leaderboard.
- Players who drop a league on their first load are expected: gates rose, trophy counts didn't.
