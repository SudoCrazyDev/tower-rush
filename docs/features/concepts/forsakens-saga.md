## 1. The saga spine

- **Book 1, "The Chosen":** the Keeper stops the first corruption. The Portal Wizard falls, laughing:
  "The Chaos Corruption has already begun."
- **Book 2, "Chaos Corrupted"** (5 stories): every land has a guardian and an Orb, its heart. In each
  land the chaos finds a hero with a **righteous wound** and twists that hero's sense of justice. When
  the Keeper cleanses an Orb, a **violet shard** escapes it. A hidden hand collects the shards.
- **Book 3** (5 stories, title TBD): the shards are used. Chaos breaks loose across every land.
  **Book 3 Story 5 reveals the mastermind** (see section 6).

### The justice thread
Each fallen hero holds one twisted face of justice. Together they foreshadow the mastermind, a being
torn between the justice of Good and the justice of Evil.

| Story | Land | Fallen hero | Twisted justice |
|---|---|---|---|
| B2 S1 | Elven Wilds | Vaeltharion | **Recognition:** "My people deserve to be seen." |
| B2 S2 | Drowned Coast | Corvaline | **Protection:** "No one will ever be taken again." |
| B2 S3 | Sunspire Desert | Sefa the Chainbreaker | **Equality:** "No one will ever kneel again." |
| B2 S4 | Emberhold | Thane Durgrim | **Vengeance:** "An oath broken must be paid in blood." |
| B2 S5 | The Silver Tribunal | High Justiciar Argent | **Law without mercy:** "Every wrong is judged." |

### The shard count
One shard per story. The Story 2 cliffhanger says "Two hearts taken. Three remain." The Book 2 finale
completes all five.

### Foreshadowing rules (until Book 3 Story 5)
- Never show the mastermind's face or say its name.
- Show only fragments: a gloved hand, a **broken pair of scales**, a voice with two tones (one calm,
  one cold).
- Every fallen hero heard "a voice" offering them justice. Their descriptions of it should match.

### Story shapes: never the same story twice
Story 1's shape (waves, guardian boss, 20-wave gauntlet with a rally) is the baseline. Story 2 keeps
it and freshens it with the tide. From Story 3 on, every story breaks it in its own way.

| Story | Ch 1 | Ch 2 | Ch 3 (and 4) | The guardian is... | Fallen hero's fate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Waves + captain | Guardian boss | Gauntlet + rally | the boss | dies |
| S2 | Waves + tide pools | Guardian on a rolling isle | Gauntlet + the Pearl reveal | the boss | lives, reunited |
| S3 | **Who is the enemy?** (masked crowd) | **Arena duel** vs an AI deck | **Purify, don't kill** | **the prisoner** you free | chooses to become stone |
| S4 | **Flashback:** play the betrayed dwarves | **Moving siege boss** across the whole chapter | **Hero turns ally** vs a new threat | **the weapon** | lives, joins your side mid-story |
| S5 | **Trial with pleas** (player choice) | **Prison break:** start with no deck | Ch 3: **survive the Scale** (no boss) · Ch 4: **collapsing board** finale | **the arena** itself | lives, broken by the reveal |

Rules: no two stories share a chapter format. Guardians change role every story. Each new mechanic
belongs to one story and changes how you place units or build the deck.

## 2. Book 2 Story 2: "The Drowned Coast"

**Logline:** The sea has stopped moving. A grieving lighthouse keeper holds the tide still with a
stolen Orb, so that the sea can never take anyone again. She does not know that the daughter she
mourns is alive.

### Bridge from Story 1
In Story 1 panel 14, green light washes the chaos out of the Forest Orb. Story 2 opens on what that
light hid: one violet shard escapes and streaks west toward the sea. Queen Aelyria sends the Keeper
after it.

### Cast
- **Corvaline, Keeper of the Lantern** (fallen hero). She has kept the Saltmere lighthouse for 40
  years. A storm took her daughter Maren. A voice told her: *hold the tide, and the sea takes no one
  ever again.* She set the Tide Orb in her lamp. Its beam turns whatever it touches violet.
- **Ondrath, the Sleeping Isle** (guardian). An ancient sea turtle with an island on its back. The
  Orb was torn from its shell. It lies corrupted in the drained bay, crusted with violet barnacles.
- **Pearl** (guide, then twist). A young merfolk scout who leads the Keeper. She is quiet about her
  past and afraid of the lighthouse. She is Maren.
- **The Drowned Crew** (captains): **Bosun Grell** (anchor) and **Lusca the Siren** (sleep song).

### Chapters
| # | Chapter | Waves | Boss | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Stranded Harbor | 10 | Bosun Grell | The bay is drained mud and ships lie beached. Merfolk gasp in shrinking pools while corrupted crabs and drowned sailors crawl toward town. |
| 2 | Ondrath, the Sleeping Isle | 1 (boss only) | Ondrath | The fight is on the turtle's back, and the island rolls. Cleansed, Ondrath says: "She did not steal it from greed. She stole it from grief." |
| 3 | The Lantern at World's Edge | 20 | Grell, Lusca, Corvaline | On the causeway, Corvaline releases the sea in surges. Mid-boss, Pearl cries "Mother!", and Corvaline falters (a stunned phase). Rally: Ondrath rises and the tide returns, carrying the merfolk in. |

### Ending (draft beats)
1. The lantern cracks. Corvaline kneels on the wet stone.
2. Maren takes her hand: "The sea didn't take me, Mother. It kept me."
3. Corvaline: "I held the whole ocean still... and you were in it all along."
4. The Tide Orb sinks back into Ondrath's shell, and the waves breathe again.
5. Unlike Vaeltharion, Corvaline lives. She relights the lamp with plain fire.
6. Cliffhanger: two violet shards meet in the dark, and a gloved hand closes over them. "Two hearts
   taken. Three remain." To be continued.

### New mechanics
| Mechanic | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Tide cycle** (headline) | Ch 1, 3 | A tide meter swings between low and high. At low tide, extra mudflat slots open and monsters slog slowly. At high tide, low slots flood and units on them are *submerged* (no attacks), while monsters swim faster. |
| **Tide pools** | Ch 1 | Side objective: a monster that reaches a pool drains it. Each pool saved adds a bonus to the first-clear reward. |
| **Rolling isle** | Ch 2 | When Ondrath thrashes, the path shifts for a few seconds. |
| **Anchor hook** | Bosun Grell | Yanks one unit to a random other slot. |
| **Siren song** | Lusca | Puts 1-2 units to sleep until the player taps them awake. |
| **Lantern beam** | Corvaline | A visible beam sweeps the board. Units in it are *glimmered* (weaker attacks for a few seconds). |

**Rewards:** story card **Maren, the Tide-Born** (stronger at high tide) and the badge **Tidewarden**.

## 3. Book 2 Story 3: "The Throne of Dust"

**Logline:** A freed gladiator overthrew a cruel sultan and swore that no one would ever kneel
again. The chaos made her vow literal: the whole desert city has been ground down into identical
Faceless. Soldier and civilian look exactly the same. Only one throne still stands, and she sits on
it.

**Shape:** you can't tell friend from foe, then you fight a former ally in the arena, then you must
save the villain instead of killing her. The guardian is not a boss. She is a prisoner.

### Bridge from Story 2
The second shard flies south over the sea. At noon the desert sky turns violet. Caravans walk out of
the dunes in step, with faces as smooth as sandstone.

### Cast
- **Sefa the Chainbreaker** (fallen hero). A gladiator of the arena of **Sunspire** who led the slave
  revolt and threw down the old sultan. A voice told her: *all are equal before justice.* With the
  **Sun Orb** she made everyone equal: the **Faceless**, all the same height and face, all kneeling
  to the sand. Violet chains of light bind her to the throne. She thinks they are a crown.
- **Nefrael, the Riddle Sphinx** (guardian, prisoner). She refused to give up the Orb, so Sefa caged
  her above the arena as a trophy. She is not corrupted. She helps the Keeper with riddles shouted
  from the cage.
- **Bako** (guide). He fought beside Sefa in the arena and in the revolt. He fled when the grinding
  began, and he still loves her.
- **Captains:** **The Faceless Herald** (copies the Keeper's strongest unit) and **The Dune Warden**
  (a sand-wyrm rider who burrows monsters past your line).

### Chapters
| # | Chapter | Format | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Glass Road | 10 waves, boss: Faceless Herald. **Who is the enemy?** | Columns of Faceless march out of a sandstorm toward the last free oasis. Some are soldiers, and some are enslaved townsfolk forced to walk with them. They look identical. Bako: "Somewhere in that line is my sister." |
| 2 | The Games of Sunspire | **Arena duel**, best of 3 rounds | Sefa captures Bako and makes him fight the Keeper in the arena. Bako's AI plays a fixed deck against you, built on the PvP mode. The crowd roars for flashy play. In round 3 Bako throws the match to save the Keeper, and Sefa orders him buried in the sand. From the cage, Nefrael shouts: "Free me, and I will free him." |
| 3 | The Throne of Dust | 15 waves, then Sefa. **Purify, don't kill** | Free the sphinx while the Faceless flood the arena. Then face Sefa, who cannot be killed, only unchained. Rally: Nefrael breaks out, and the freed slaves tear off their masks and fight as themselves. |

### Ending (draft beats)
1. The last chain breaks. Sefa looks down at the Faceless kneeling all around her throne.
2. Bako, dug out of the sand: "You broke the chains so no one would kneel. Look who is kneeling."
3. Sefa steps off the throne and sits in the sand with her people.
4. Nefrael takes back the Orb and asks her true riddle: "What makes all people equal, yet no two the
   same?" Bako: "A name." Faces return, each one different.
5. The cracked city needs holding. Sefa gives herself to it: she becomes a sandstone statue at the
   arena gate, holding the walls together.
6. Sefa's last words: "The voice said all are equal before justice. It never said whose justice."
7. Cliffhanger: the third shard streaks north toward the mountains, and the ground begins to shake.
   "Three hearts taken. Two remain." To be continued.

### New mechanics
| Mechanic | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Masks and braziers** (headline) | Ch 1, 3 | Faceless walkers are **masked** and cannot be targeted. A unit placed on a **brazier slot** lights it, and every Faceless that passes a lit brazier is unmasked. Soldiers then fight. Civilians walk free and reaching the exit costs nothing, but each one hit by splash damage lowers the first-clear reward. Placement decides who you can see. |
| **Arena duel** | Ch 2 | A best-of-3 match against Bako's fixed AI deck, built on the PvP mode. |
| **Crowd favor** | Ch 2 | Multi-kills and rounds with no leaks fill a crowd meter. A full meter makes the crowd throw gold or a free unit into the arena. |
| **Purify** | Sefa | Sefa has no HP bar, only a **Chains** bar. Her body blocks all damage. Violet chains appear on her one at a time and must be targeted and broken. |
| **The cage** | Ch 3 | Nefrael's cage sits beside the path. Units next to it chip the lock between waves. Free her before the boss and she joins the rally with a stunning roar. Fail and the rally is smaller. |
| **Equal ground** | Sefa | Her aura sets every unit to the board's **average** level for a few seconds. One strong carry suffers; an evenly upgraded deck shrugs it off. |
| **Mimic** | Faceless Herald | Copies your strongest unit as a faceless monster with its attack. |

**Rewards:** story card **Bako, Chainbreaker** (stronger for each unit on the board at his level) and
the badge **Dunewarden**.

## 4. Book 2 Story 4: "The Iron Oath"

**Logline:** Deep under the mountains, the dwarves remember a promise that humans forgot. Their Thane
has lit the Forge Orb with chaos and set a walking forge marching on the human kingdoms to collect a
debt three centuries old. This time the Keeper's side is in the wrong, and the player lives through
the betrayal first.

**Shape:** a playable flashback, then one huge moving boss for a whole chapter, then the villain
fights beside you against what he unleashed. The guardian is not fought as a creature. It has been
turned into a weapon.

### Bridge from Story 3
The third shard flies north into the mountains. In the Book 1 lands, villagers report iron footprints
the size of houses. Something is marching out of the mountains toward the human kingdoms.

### Cast
- **Thane Durgrim Emberhold** (fallen hero). Long ago the human crown swore the **Iron Oath**: dwarven
  steel to win a war, in exchange for defending Emberhold forever. When dragons came, no human army
  came, and half the city burned. A voice told Durgrim: *an oath broken must be paid.* He fed the
  Forge Orb to the mountain's heart.
- **The Anvil-Heart** (guardian, weapon). The living forge-spirit of the mountain. Durgrim chained it
  inside an iron fortress on legs, the **Walking Forge**, which crawls toward the human lands
  hammering out soldiers as it goes.
- **Brakka Emberhold** (guide). Durgrim's daughter and the city's best smith. She wants her father
  back, not the debt paid, and she knows every bolt of the Walking Forge.
- **The Knights of the Candy Crown** (returning from Book 1). They are bound by the oath their
  ancestors broke, though they did not know it.

### Chapters
| # | Chapter | Format | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Three Hundred Years Ago | **Flashback**, 10 waves, sepia tint, fixed dwarf Event deck | The player is young Durgrim defending Emberhold against dragons. Each wave a horn calls for the human army, and no one answers. The goal is not to win but to **evacuate**: get enough families out before the city falls. Last bark: "They swore... they're not coming." The intro of chapter 2 shows the city burning. |
| 2 | The Walking Forge | **One moving boss across the whole chapter** | Back in the present, the Walking Forge crawls down the path toward the human lands. It has parts: legs, cannons, furnace, and the Anvil-Heart at its core. If it reaches the end, you lose. Brakka calls out which part to break. |
| 3 | Oathbreakers | 15 waves, then the Colossus. **Hero turns ally** | Before the battle, the Candy Crown knights kneel to Durgrim: "Our oath was broken. We are here to keep it now." Durgrim falters. After wave 15 the chaos tears out of him into every piece of iron on the field. The iron fuses into the **Chaos-Iron Colossus**, which attacks dwarves and humans alike. Durgrim is a **hero unit** in a fixed deck slot (he starts on the board). The rest is an Event deck that needs at least 1 knight and 1 dwarf. Rally: Emberhold dwarves and Candy Crown knights side by side. |

**Panels (17):** 4 intro (the shard flies north, the iron footprints, then two flashback setups: the
gates of Emberhold and the Iron Oath), 3 between Ch 1 and Ch 2 (the city burning, the broken oath, the
Forge in the present), 3 between Ch 2 and the Colossus (the knights kneel, Durgrim falters, the Colossus
forms) and 7 for the ending. Slugs are in the [v2.3 design doc](../v2.3-the-iron-oath/README.md).

### Ending (draft beats)
1. The Colossus falls apart into cold scrap. Durgrim stands among the knights, breathing hard.
2. Brakka: "You wanted them to pay. They came. That was the debt."
3. The freed Anvil-Heart takes the Forge Orb back, and it cools from violet to ember-gold.
4. Durgrim and the knights forge a new oath: dwarven steel and a human shield, both kept.
5. Durgrim, troubled: "The voice spoke of justice. It sounded fair... and then it sounded cold."
6. Cliffhanger, in two panels: four shards hover over a **broken pair of scales**, one pan glowing
   white and the other black; then a gloved hand closes over them. "One more heart. Then they will
   all be judged." To be continued.

### New mechanics
| Mechanic | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Evacuation** (headline) | Ch 1 | The win condition is not survival. Dwarf families walk out along a second route, and you win by getting a set number out (14 of 22). Dragons dive for the families, not for your units. |
| **Walking boss** (headline) | Ch 2 | One boss for the whole chapter, moving slowly down the path. Each part has its own HP: breaking the **legs** slows it, the **cannons** stop shelling your units, the **furnace** stops it spawning soldiers, and the **core** ends the fight. You choose which order to break them. |
| **Rune shields** | Ch 2, 3 | Iron enemies carry a rune of one element, and only units of that element break it. Deck building matters. |
| **Hero unit** | Ch 3 | Durgrim takes a fixed slot: a strong dwarf hero who starts on the board (you can drag him, but he is not in the deck, so he is never summoned or merged). The rest is an Event deck that needs at least 1 knight and 1 dwarf. |
| **Chaos-Iron Colossus** | Ch 3 | It absorbs every iron enemy you leave alive and grows stronger for each one. Clearing iron fast keeps it small. |

**Rewards:** story card **Brakka, Oathsmith** (tempers the units around her), the two new dwarf cards
(**Emberhold Gunner** and **Runecaller**), and the badge **Oathkeeper**.

## 5. Book 2 Story 5: "The Silver Tribunal" (Book 2 finale, 4 chapters)

**Status:** approved pitch, built in v2.4.0 (see [v2.4](../v2.4-the-silver-tribunal/README.md)).
**No angels or divine beings anywhere in this story**: the user reserves angels for a later series.

**Logline:** Aurelion is a floating city of clockwork law. A long-vanished artificer people built
brass judges to give the world justice without bias; they have ruled a thousand years and the city
flies on law-engines. The judge is incorruptible, which is exactly what the chaos used.

**Shape:** a courtroom where the player chooses a plea for each charge, a prison break with a
confiscated deck, a chapter with no boss where the arena itself judges you, and a finale on a city
falling out of the sky. The guardian is the board itself.

### Bridge from Story 4
A silver light pulls the Keeper up into the sky. They wake in chains in **Aurelion**, which guards
the last Orb: the **Orb of Verdicts**.

### Cast
- **High Justiciar Argent** (fallen hero). The oldest clockwork judge, built without mercy. She bound
  the Orb of Verdicts into her gavel-sword. She is corrupted *without turning violet*: her light is
  cold, perfect silver. (Story card: **Argent, the Merciful**, earned at the end.)
- **The Great Scale** (guardian). The brass law-engine that holds Aurelion up; the Chapter 3 board
  stands on its two pans.
- **The witnesses.** The Keeper's past allies are called to testify: Queen Aelyria, Corvaline and
  Maren, Bako, Durgrim and Brakka. Vaeltharion appears once as golden dust.
- **Bosses:** **The Prosecutor** (a reading-automaton that unrolls the charges and declares laws),
  **The Bailiff** (a chain construct) and **Argent**.

### Chapters
| # | Chapter | Format | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Trial | 8 counts (waves). **Plea choice** before each one; the last count brings the Prosecutor | Each count is a charge from one land (Elven Wilds, Drowned Coast, Sunspire, Emberhold: two counts each), and its wave is drawn from that land's monsters. A witness speaks between counts. |
| 2 | Cells of Aurelion | 12 waves, boss: The Bailiff. **Prison break** | The verdict is guilty whatever you pleaded. Your deck is confiscated, and you start with one unit. Your other cards are locked in cells along the path. Break a cell to get that card back mid-battle. |
| 3 | The Great Scale | 12 waves, **no boss** | The board stands on the Scale's two pans. Survive while keeping it level. At the end the Scale weighs the Keeper and settles even: "Neither guiltless nor guilty. Human." The judgment cracks Aurelion's foundation, and the city begins to fall. |
| 4 | Verdict | 20 waves, then Argent. **Collapsing board** | Argent passes sentence as the city falls apart around you. Mid-boss, Vaeltharion's golden dust speaks: "Justice without mercy is only another chaos." Argent falters. Rally: every Book 2 ally charges at once. |

### Ending beats
1. Argent's gavel-sword shatters. For the first time in a thousand years a tear runs down her brass
   face: quicksilver.
2. The Orb of Verdicts is cleansed, and the fifth shard tears free.
3. The Keeper and Aelyria watch five shards fly to a **broken pair of scales** in the sky.
4. The two-toned voice, heard openly for the first time: "Good failed them. Evil failed them. I am
   the balance they forgot."
5. Violet chaos pours onto **every land at once**: the Candy Kingdom, the Elven Wilds, the Drowned
   Coast, Sunspire, Emberhold.
6. Argent, kneeling: "The voice I obeyed... it once sat beside me in this court."
7. "End of Book 2" title card.

### New mechanics
| Mechanic | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Pleas** (headline) | Ch 1 | Before each count: **Guilty** makes the wave harder (more HP), but that land's witness fights for you that wave. **Not guilty** keeps the wave normal, but the Prosecutor declares a **law** for it ("No fire", "Only four may strike", "No merging", "No summoning after the bell"). A unit that breaks the law is jailed (disabled) for the wave. The first player choice in story mode; replays let players try the other plea. |
| **Confiscated deck** | Ch 2 | You start with 1 unit. Cells along the path hold your other cards. Killing monsters next to a cell breaks it and returns the card to your hand. |
| **Chains** | The Bailiff | Chains a unit in place: it cannot be moved or merged until the chain breaks (its attacks and your taps wear it down). |
| **Balance** | Ch 3 | The board is two pans. The pan with more total unit ranks tilts down, and its units take crushing hits (stunned). Waves come down both sides, so build evenly. |
| **Collapsing board** | Ch 4 | Every few waves a section of the city falls away. Units on it are caught by a free tile, or returned as mana. The board shrinks toward the final duel. |
| **Witness deck** | Ch 4 | An Event deck built from every Book 2 story card. |
| **Silver light** | Argent | Effects that only hurt chaos (the Lance Knight's bane) do nothing to her. A sentence counter fills, and when it is full she *condemns* the strongest unit on the board. Her mercy is the answer: beside her card, no unit is jailed, chained or condemned. |

**Rewards:** a mythic chest, the card **Argent, the Merciful**, the badge **Unbroken** (finished
Book 2). A card back showing the broken scales is open: the game has no card-back system yet.

## 6. Book 3: constraints for the finale

- **Story 5 reveals the mastermind:** a being once torn between the justice of Good and the justice
  of Evil. It could choose neither, and that division spawned **Chaos** itself.
- Its symbol is the **broken pair of scales** (white pan, black pan), seeded in Book 2 Stories 4-5.
- Its voice has two tones, and the fallen heroes all describe it the same way.
- It once sat in the Silver Tribunal beside Argent (Book 2 Story 5, panel 6).
- Open question: is it a new character or an existing unit in the roster? If it is an existing unit,
  its card and description need quiet hints from Book 2 onward.
- Book 3 Stories 1-4: TBD. Natural setup: the lands of Books 1-2 fall again, and the Keeper gathers
  the healed heroes (Aelyria, Corvaline, Bako, Durgrim and Argent) as allies.
- Sefa can wake from her statue in Book 3 (decided 2026-10-10).
