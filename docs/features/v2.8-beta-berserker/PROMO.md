## Facebook / Discord announcement (long)

> **Crown & Keep 2.8: Beta Berserker is live!** 🪓
>
> We're officially in Beta, and the Orcs are here to celebrate.

> (PvP lives there now), and every Mythic throws its own projectile.

## Discord patch notes (short)

**v2.8.0 Beta Berserker**
- 5 Orc units: Peon, Grunt, Headhunter, Spirit Walker, Kodo Beasts (they never awaken)
- New neutral element: Normal
- Unit Lab open to everyone (unowned cards are locked)
- Lobby mode carousel: Arena, Stories, PvP, Unit Lab (the PvP button is gone)
- Mythic projectiles for Void Titan, Dragon Egg, Chrono Mage and Monkey King
- Balance: Crystal Golem awakened speed x0.2 to x0.4, Hourglass Owl charge 30% to 40%, Sand Worm damage 44 to 47, Sand Monk damage 15.8 to 19
- Fixes: the Summon button no longer shrinks, smoother late waves (cooler phones), new Slow and Frozen effects
