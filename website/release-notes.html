The Silver Tribunal

The Book 2 finale. In Aurelion, a floating city of clockwork law, brass judges put the Keeper on trial.

Highlights

  • Story 5: The Silver Tribunal. Four chapters that unlock after The Iron Oath: The Trial, Cells of Aurelion, The Great Scale and Verdict.
  • Pleas and laws. Before each count you plead. Guilty makes the monsters tougher but brings a witness to fight beside you. Not guilty declares a law, and units that break it are jailed.
  • A confiscated deck. In Cells of Aurelion you start with one card. Defeat monsters near the cells to win the other four back.
  • Chains, the Great Scale and a falling city. The Bailiff chains your units, the Scale crushes the heavier pan, and in Verdict the city collapses tile by tile.
  • New Event card: Argent, the Merciful. Beside her, no unit is jailed, chained or condemned.
  • 4 new bosses (the Prosecutor, the Bailiff, Vaeltharion's dust and Argent), 5 new monsters, 4 new arenas and the Unbroken badge for finishing Book 2.

Hotfixes

v2.4.1
Story fixes and balance Oct 12
  • The Throne of Dust, chapter 3: any unit can now be dragged onto an empty tile, so you can reach the braziers.
  • The Iron Oath, chapter 1: families no longer die at the start of the road. Dragons only dive on a family ahead of them, and whelps wait longer before their first dive.
  • The Walking Forge: the part bars can be tapped on desktop. Its iron minions have a tenth of their HP, and the Forge walks slower.
  • The Walking Forge: breaking the legs stops it dead, but its furnace then spawns iron soldiers every 2 s. The legs repair themselves over 55 s, a little weaker each time. The cannons and furnace have less HP.
  • The Chaos-Iron Colossus grows 5% per iron monster it eats (was 4%).
  • The Event deck pick screen no longer shows "(undefined)" for dwarf units.
v2.3.0 Oct 11, 2026

The Iron Oath

Three hundred years ago the crown swore to defend the dwarves of Emberhold, then broke its word. Now the dwarves collect.

Highlights

  • Story 4: The Iron Oath. Three chapters that unlock after The Throne of Dust: Three Hundred Years Ago, The Walking Forge and Oathbreakers.
  • Evacuation. Get the dwarf families out of burning Emberhold while dragons dive at them.
  • The Walking Forge. One boss for the whole chapter. Tap its legs, cannons or furnace to aim every unit at that part, then free the Anvil-Heart.
  • Rune shields. Iron monsters carry runes that only units of the matching element can break.
  • The Chaos-Iron Colossus eats the iron monsters around it and grows with each one.
  • New cards: Brakka Emberhold (Event), plus Emberhold Gunner and Runecaller as story rewards. Durgrim fights as a story hero, and finishing the story earns the Oathkeeper badge.
v2.2.0 Oct 11, 2026

Sand & Sea

Book 2 continues with two new stories: a drowned coast held by a stolen tide, and a desert city ground down into the Faceless.

Highlights

  • Story 2: The Drowned Coast. Three chapters with a tide cycle that floods board slots at high tide, tide pools as a side objective, a "cleanse the shell" fight against Ondrath, and Corvaline shielded by lanterns until your units break them.
  • Story 3: The Throne of Dust. Masked Faceless that braziers unmask, civilians to save, a best-of-3 arena duel against Bako's deck with a crowd favor meter, Sefa's 6-chain bar instead of HP, and Nefrael to free from a cage.
  • 8 new bosses: Grell, Lusca, Ondrath, Corvaline, Faceless Herald, Dune Warden, Sefa and Nefrael, plus about 12 new monsters.
  • New boss skills: anchor hook, siren song (tap your units to wake them), lantern beam, mimic, burrow and equal ground.
  • 2 new Event cards: Maren, the Tide-Born and Bako, Chainbreaker. They don't drop from chests; level them by replaying chapters.
  • 6 new arenas: 3 coast arenas whose flooded high-tide art fades in, and 3 desert arenas.
  • 2 new badges: Tidewarden and Dunewarden.

Hotfixes

v2.2.1
Balance Oct 11
  • Rogue Knight crit chance cut from 95% to 20%. It was dealing about 2.5× the damage of other Epics.
  • Ondrath's barnacles are much tougher (50k, 70k and 110k HP) and spawn a minion when hit.
  • Corvaline's ally charge is gone. Her lanterns now ward her until your units break them, each lantern is tougher than the last, and she hurries while unwarded.
v2.1.0 Oct 10, 2026

Chaos Corrupted

Book 2 of "The Forsakens" saga begins as the corruption reaches the elven forests.

Highlights

  • Story mode becomes a saga. Books now sit under "The Forsakens". Book 2 unlocks once you finish Book 1.
  • Story 1: The Elven Wilds. The Fleeing Grove (10 waves, boss Captain Morvane), Thalmyr, the Torn Guardian (a boss-only fight) and the Summit of Vaeltharion (20 waves, three captains, then the Elven Commander).
  • 5 new bosses: Morvane, Sylris, Kaelen, Thalmyr and Vaeltharion, plus 5 corrupted elf monsters.
  • New boss skills: entangle (units can't attack until the wave ends), impale, sapling trail, arrow volley and block.
  • Queen Aelyria's charge. Mid-battle, her army storms Vaeltharion, pushes him back and takes 50% of his HP.
  • New art: 3 arenas, 16 story panels, 8 portraits and 5 ally sprites, plus the Wilds Warden badge.
v2.0.0 Oct 9, 2026

The Grand Revamp

One combat engine for every mode, units with several archetypes and perks, and a full visual refresh.

Highlights

  • One combat engine. Solo battles now run on the same engine as PvP, so the rules are the same everywhere.
  • Deeper units. A unit can combine several archetypes (say, a sniper that also executes) and any number of perks, each with its own values.
  • New icons: 26 archetype icons, 7 perk icons, 6 element emblems and 18 race crests.
  • New effects for projectiles, impacts and statuses, plus generic and signature weapons with melee strike effects.
  • UI polish: labelled card details, a race crest on each card corner, a framed boss intro card, livelier lobby mode cards and cleaner buttons.
v1.3.0 Oct 6, 2026

Branding Revamp

Tower Rush is now Crown & Keep: Tower Defense. Same game, new name.

Highlights

  • A new name, because "Tower Rush" was already taken.
  • A new logo in the same style: CROWN & KEEP with a shield "&" and a TOWER DEFENSE ribbon.
  • A new app icon (a crowned gold shield with a keep), favicon and share images.
  • A What's New popup announces the rename in game.
  • Nothing lost. Logins, settings and progress all carry over. Colours and art style stay the same.

v1.2.0 Oct 6, 2026

Stories

The Candy Kingdom is falling to chaos. Save its princess, then find out who corrupted it.

Highlights

  • Story mode, Book 1 "The Chosen". 3 stories of 3 chapters each (10, 15 and 20 waves), with illustrated story panels, boss fights and 1–3 stars per chapter.
  • Saving the Muse rewards Princess Muse, the first Event card. Her Last Call aura boosts attack speed and damage for units in a 3×3 square.
  • Chaorruption: pick 5 of 9 Knights and Mercenaries and face the Chaos Jawbreaker. Rewards: Pentagonal Knight and Rogue Knight (Epic).
  • The Beginning: your own deck, both knights required, no Legendary or Mythic units, and a cliffhanger ending. Rewards: a Legendary chest and "The Chosen" badge.
  • 19 new monsters and 8 new bosses with charm, roar, split, layered phases and portals.
  • New statuses: Rally, Irritation, Fatigue and Shellshock.
  • Lobby redesign with swipeable Arena and Stories mode cards, and book and chapter carousels.
