Beta Release 2.8

Beta Berserker

The Orcs march in. Five Orc units arrive with a new Normal element. The Unit Lab opens to everyone, the lobby gets a mode carousel, and every Mythic throws its own projectile.

New units

The Orcs

Five Orcs, all of the Normal element. Orcs never awaken: their power grows in battle instead.

Peon

CommonSupport+3 mana / 2s

Pillage

Never attacks. Makes 3 mana every 2 s. Each pulse has a 50% chance to pay double. Every other Orc on the board adds 2% × its rank to that chance (up to 100%).

Grunt

Damage 35Speed 0.9/sPassive

Berserker Strength

Throws an axe at the leading monster. The longer he stands, the harder he hits: ×2 damage after 10 waves, ×4 after 20, ×7 after 30, then ×1.5 more every 5 waves. A merge starts the count over; moving him keeps it. His axe changes with every tier.

Headhunter

Damage 25Speed 1.7/sPassive

Berserker Upgrade

Throws spears at random monsters. A monster 87% of the way to your keep draws his aim for ×5.2 damage. From wave 20 he attacks ×3.2 faster.

Spirit Walker

Damage 50Speed 0.6/sPassive

Ancestral Spirit

Throws a spirit lantern at the leading monster. Each hit has a 76% chance to push the target and up to 4 monsters near it back 35px. Bosses are pushed half as far.

Kodo Beasts

RareSupportAlways on

War Drums

Never attacks. Adjacent units attack ×1.2 faster. When a monster is halfway down its path, ×2.4. When one reaches 88%, every unit on the board attacks ×3 faster. Several Kodos don't stack: the best bonus counts.

Witch Doctor is an Orc too, so he counts for the Peon's Pillage.

New element

Normal

  • Neutral. No monster resists Normal damage and none is weak to it.
  • Find it in the Deck. Normal has its own grey icon and its own filter chip.
For everyone

Unit Lab

The Unit Lab is open to every player.

  • Test your own cards. Units you don't own yet show greyed out with a lock.
  • No stakes. Lab battles give no rewards, trophies or quest progress.
Lobby

Mode carousel

  • One carousel for Arena, Stories, PvP and the Unit Lab. The cards next to the current one peek in at the sides.
  • Swipe, tap the arrows or tap a side card to move. The page dots stay.
  • PvP moves into the carousel. The separate PvP button is gone. Deck and Shop stay where they were.
New visuals

Mythic projectiles

  • Void Titan tears a void rift.
  • Dragon Egg breathes dragon flame.
  • Chrono Mage throws a spinning clockwork gear.
  • Monkey King leaves a golden cloud trail, with a new impact on his staff.
  • Each one has its own impact effect, too.
Unit balance

Balance changes

  • Crystal Golem: awakened speed ×0.2 → ×0.4.
  • Hourglass Owl: ultimate charge 30% → 40%.
  • Sand Worm: damage 44 → 47.
  • Sand Monk: damage 15.8 → 19.
Polish

Fixes and smoother battles

  • Summon button. Fast taps no longer shrink it a little more each time.
  • Smoother late waves. Fewer effects pile up at once, so battles past wave 20 stay smooth and phones stay cooler.
  • New Slow and Frozen effects on monsters.
  • Ability effects for the Orcs: Grunt's rage aura grows with every tier, Headhunter's frenzy and focus shots stand out, and War Drums shows which units it speeds up.
