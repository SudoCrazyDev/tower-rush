Release 2.7.0

The Great Awakening

Every unit gets a revamp, rarity by rarity. Awakened units now have their own damage, speed and a signature ultimate. Awakening is also easier to reach: the top merge rank drops from 7 to 5.

What changed

The basics

+3%Attack speed per card level (damage still +8%). Level 15 gives ×1.42 speed.
Rank 5Max merge rank drops from 7 to 5. Awakening needs 15 summons instead of 63.
Own ultimateEach awakened unit has its own damage and speed boost and its own ultimate.
  • Card levels. Damage still rises +8% per level. Attack speed now rises +3% per level too.
  • Max rank is 5. Peak rank damage is ×5 instead of ×7. The faster road to awakening is worth more than the lost ceiling.
  • Timer ultimates fire every set number of seconds. Hourglass Owls charge them faster, and Echo Spirits repeat them at echo strength.
  • Passive ultimates change every attack. They have no charge and can't be echoed (Hourglass Owls give attack speed instead).
  • Always on ultimates work all battle long, with no charge and no echo.
  • Units without awakened art keep the old awakening: ×1.5 damage, ×1.2 speed and a radius strike every 10 s for ×4 damage.
  • Awakened card backs. Flip an awakened card to see a panel with its damage and speed boosts and its ultimate, with a cooldown pill (EVERY 15s, PASSIVE or ALWAYS ON).
  • New visuals. Every awakened unit has a new ultimate effect. Crystal Golem and Plague Alchemist have new awakened art, and Tide Mermaid has new awakened animations.

In the lists below, Damage and Speed are the awakened multipliers on the unit's base stats.

New rules

Rare reworks

Four rares change how they work. The numbers on their cards match.

Frog Alchemist

Never awakens. Each attack drops a poison puddle (radius 70 px) that deals his hit per second. It lasts 5 s, +0.2 s per card level above 1, +0.5 s per in-battle power-up. Damage rises with rank (it is his hit). Overlapping puddles don't stack.

Raccoon Thief

Base damage 10. A mana pulse comes every 6 s (0.1 s faster per card level above 1, min 3 s). It pays 5 mana, +2 per rank above 1, +3 per power-up.

Vine Druid

Never attacks. Grows one prickly vine ahead of the front monster. It holds monsters for 3 s (bosses 1 s) and deals her hit per second to monsters in it. Regrows 6 s after it withers.

Snowglobe Fairy

Base freeze chance is 20% (was 12%). It still rises with rank.

New lobby mode

Unit Lab

A third lobby card next to Arena and Stories: pick any units and test them on the meadow.

  • Pick any units you like, then try them out with no stakes.
  • Tap to place units on any tile, and move them around freely.
  • Next-wave picker. Jump around with -10, -1, +1 and +10, then press GO TO WAVE N.
