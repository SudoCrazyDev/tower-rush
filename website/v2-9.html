Release 2.9

League Revamp

Trophies must be earned now. Every league has a minimum wave: reach it and you win trophies, fall short and you lose them. The gates are longer, two new leagues sit above Champion, and one more league is never spoken of. You can also open any player's profile to see their deck and badges, and the leagues list shows who sits on top of each league.

Arena runs

Win them or lose them

  • A minimum wave per league. Reach it and the run wins 5 trophies, plus more for every wave past it.
  • No cap on gains. A great deck that goes deep earns as much as it can reach.
  • Fall short and you lose trophies. The further short, the more you lose, up to your league's limit.
  • Gold League examples (minimum wave 12): wave 40 wins +61, wave 12 wins +5, wave 8 loses −17.
  • Ranked PvP is unchanged.
Longer gates

The league ladder

Arena lock

Your two highest arenas

  • Trophies count only in your two highest unlocked arenas. Lower arenas still pay coins and gems, but never trophies.
  • The arena card tells you. It shows the wave you need, or "Gold only" when that arena gives no trophies.
Fair play

Leaving a run

  • A run you leave counts as a loss. Close the game mid-run, or start playing on another device, and that run takes your league's biggest loss when your next run starts.
  • Stories are not affected.
New

Player profiles

Every player now has a character page. See how the best players build their decks.

  • Open any profile. Tap a player on the leaderboard, in an arena's top players, or at the top of a league. Tap your own avatar in the lobby for yours.
  • Their deck on stage. The hero and all 5 deck units idle on the arena they last played, with each unit's level.
  • Their record. Trophies, best wave, ranked rating and win-loss, and how long they've been playing.
  • Badges. Every league they've reached, their peak ranked tier and the story badges they've earned.
Redesigned

The leagues list

  • One card per league, in its own colours, with its gate, the wave you need and its promotion reward. Scroll from Bronze up to Immortal.
  • Who's on top. Each card shows the player leading that league right now. Tap them to open their profile.
  • Your league stands out. It opens first and wears its badge's wings.
