How to play

Every battle is a race: build your board faster than the horde can reach the gate.

1

Summon

Spend mana to drop a random unit from your 5-card deck onto the board.

2

Merge

Drag two matching units together to rank up. Higher ranks hit much harder.

3

Power up

Boost a card mid-battle, call your hero's ability, and awaken your best units.

4

Defend

Survive the waves, beat the boss and climb the trophy road to new arenas.

Real-time

PvP battles

Same waves, same clock, two players. Spend mana to send monsters to your rival's board and build income to outlast them.

  • Ranked climb a rating ladder with its own tiers
  • Mirror both players get the same deck
  • Casual and VS Bot for practice
  • Friend codes to challenge anyone
Core

Arena battles

Survive as many waves as you can. Your best wave per arena, daily quests, login rewards and chests keep every run worth it.

Real-time PvP

A duel, up close

Two boards, the same waves on the same clock. Hold your keep and flood your rival's with monsters. This is a real match, recorded in the game.

    Climb the ranks

    Trophy leagues

    Trophies measure how far you've come. Earn them by surviving waves in arena battles and by winning Ranked PvP. Pass a league's trophy mark and you move up and collect its reward.

    The first time you reach a league, its reward is yours to keep. Lose trophies and you can drop back down, but you never pay the reward back. More trophies also unlock new arenas.
